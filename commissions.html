Commissions

Commission a piece

Have a place that means something — a childhood home, a stretch of coast, a city you fell in love with? I'd love to paint it for you in watercolour, made to keep.

How it works

Four gentle steps

01

Get in touch

Tell me the place, the feeling you're after, and a rough size. Photos are welcome but not required.

02

We plan it together

I'll suggest a size, palette, and approach, and share a quote and timeline before anything begins.

03

I paint it

Your piece is painted by hand on cotton paper. You'll see progress along the way.

04

It arrives

Carefully packed and ready to frame — a one-of-a-kind painting of somewhere that matters.

What I love to paint

⌂

Homes & buildings

Heritage houses, family homes, and storied architecture.

Coast & harbour
